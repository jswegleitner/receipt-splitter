| Currency conversion | ❌ | ✅ |
| Search expenses | ❌ | ✅ |
| No ads | ❌ | ✅ |

#### Strengths
- Market leader with strong brand recognition
- Robust ecosystem (iOS, Android, Web)
- Payment integrations (Venmo, PayPal)
- Debt simplification algorithms
- 100+ currency support

#### Weaknesses
- **Requires account creation**
- **OCR/itemization locked behind $60/year paywall**
- Complex UI for simple one-time splits

- App download required for best experience

#### Pricing Psychology
- Free tier is intentionally limited
- Pro features are "nice to have" not "must have"
- Subscription model creates recurring friction

## Competitive Strategy Recommendations

### 1. **Own the "No Account" Positioning**

**Messaging:**
- "No download. No signup. No problem."
- "Your friends will actually use this one."
- "The anti-app app."

**Execution:**
- Make this the headline on landing page
- Emphasize in all marketing materials
- Use as primary differentiator

---

### 2. **Compete on Speed, Not Features**

**Don't try to match:**
- Splitwise's expense history
- Tricount's payment integrations
- Long-term tracking features

**Instead, dominate:**
- Time from link click to working split
- Simplicity of adding items
- Speed of real-time sync

---

### 3. **Leverage the OCR Advantage**

**Current situation:**
- Splitwise charges $60/year for OCR
- Tricount has no OCR
- Your app offers OCR for free (with BYOK)

**Strategy:**
- Consider: Offer 3 free scans (your API key) then BYOK
- Creates trial experience
- Reduces friction for first-time users

---

### 4. **Target Splitwise's Weak Spot**

**Splitwise frustration points:**
- "I don't want to create an account for one dinner"
- "Why do I need to pay for receipt scanning?"
- "It's overkill for splitting one bill"

**Marketing approach:**
- Target "Splitwise alternative" keywords
- Create comparison content
- Emphasize simplicity for one-time use

---

### 5. **Build for Virality**

**Every split = potential new users**
- Each shared link exposes new users
- Room codes are memorable and shareable
- No friction means higher conversion

**Enhance virality:**
- Add "Split your own receipt" CTA on results page
- Make room codes even more memorable
- Consider referral rewards (free scans)
